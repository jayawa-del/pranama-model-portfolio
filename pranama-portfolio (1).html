About Me

A model who believes that fashion is art, expression, and identity — all in one.

Hi, I'm Pranama Jayawardhane — A fashion model based in Sri Lanka with a deep love for storytelling through style. I bring a unique blend of elegance, strength, and versatility to every shoot and runway appearance.

Whether it's an intimate editorial, a commercial campaign, or the raw energy of a live runway show, I give every project my complete dedication and creative presence. My goal is always to bring the designer's vision to life — authentically and powerfully.
